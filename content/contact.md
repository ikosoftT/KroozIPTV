## Choose the conversation that matches your question
For a new order, include the duration you are considering, your intended device, and the channels or viewing categories that matter. Support should confirm current availability, total price, simultaneous connections, payment instructions, and activation timing before you pay. The [pricing page](/pricing) can start that conversation with a selected plan already included.

For a trial, identify the device and ask about current availability, duration, included access, and connection limits. A request does not automatically activate an account. Prepare your player and network using the [setup guides](/setup) so the available test time can focus on real viewing rather than avoidable installation questions.

For a channel or event, send the exact name, relevant date, your time zone, and location needed to identify the feed. A national network name may not identify a local affiliate, and a broad sports category does not establish access to a particular game. The [directory](/channels) is a list of requests rather than a verified lineup.

## Make a technical report useful
Start with the stage that fails: installing the app, entering credentials, loading the list, starting video, hearing audio, or displaying the guide. Include the exact device model and player version. Note whether every stream is affected or only one, and when the issue began.

Describe a few controlled checks rather than changing many settings at once. For example, report whether another confirmed channel works, whether Ethernet behaves differently from Wi-Fi, or whether the issue began after an update. The [troubleshooting decision tree](/blog/iptv-not-working) can help you gather this evidence.

Hide passwords, full playlist URLs, account QR codes, and sensitive payment information from screenshots. Share an order reference privately when it is needed to identify a purchase. Support does not need your card security code, banking password, or unrelated identity documents to understand a playback symptom.

## Existing orders and activation questions
Keep the order reference, agreed duration, payment confirmation, and any activation instructions together. If details have not arrived, explain when the order was confirmed and what delivery timing was agreed. This website does not promise a fixed response or activation window that has not been confirmed in the conversation.

For billing or refund questions, describe the issue and requested resolution. Read the [refund information](/refund-policy) and retain the written terms supplied before payment. A request needs review; sending a message does not itself approve a refund or establish a completion date.

WhatsApp is a third-party service. Opening a button takes you to its application or website with a prefilled message that you can review before sending. The trial selector on this site does not store your notes in a local customer database or submit a hidden support ticket.
