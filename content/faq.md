## Start with your viewing priorities
Before choosing a plan, identify the device you will use, the content you want to confirm, and whether anyone else needs simultaneous access. These details lead to a more useful conversation than asking for the largest catalog or assuming every screen uses the same player.

The [channel page](/channels) lists popular requests and categories, not a guaranteed lineup. Ask about exact feeds, local programming, languages, and event dates before paying. Content schedules and package availability can change, so a previous viewing experience is not a promise of permanent access.

Use the [device guides](/devices) to understand your app platform. A smart TV model, operating system, and player edition all matter. Third-party player charges may be separate from the subscription, and app installation does not activate an account.

## Ordering is a conversation
Choose a duration on the [pricing page](/pricing) to open WhatsApp with the plan and listed price already included. Confirm the package, connection allowance, total amount, payment instructions, activation process, and applicable terms before proceeding. There is no payment-card form or automatic checkout on this website.

For a [trial](/free-trial), support confirms availability, duration, and included access. Test the actual screen, network, sound output, captions, and guide that you intend to use. Do not assume a trial permits multiple simultaneous streams or automatically extends if setup takes longer than expected.

Keep the order reference and written agreement. If an important condition is unclear, ask before paying. The [terms](/terms), [privacy policy](/privacy-policy), and [refund information](/refund-policy) explain the website’s approach and the questions that should be confirmed in the order conversation.

## When you need more than a short answer
For pauses and buffering, use the [twelve-step checklist](/blog/iptv-buffering-fix) to change one variable at a time. For a rejected login, app crash, or blank channel, begin with the [troubleshooting decision tree](/blog/iptv-not-working). A missing program guide deserves a separate data and time-zone investigation.

Contact support with the exact model, app version, error, and affected streams. Hide account credentials in screenshots and avoid repeatedly factory-resetting a device for a single channel issue. A clear symptom report gives support a practical next step and preserves settings that already work.
