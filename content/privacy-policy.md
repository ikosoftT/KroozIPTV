## Scope of this notice
This notice describes the information flows implemented on the KroozIPTV website. The site presents plan information, editorial guidance, channel requests, and links to WhatsApp conversations. It does not provide account registration, an on-site payment form, or a customer database for trial submissions.

The service operator’s identity and any additional privacy terms applying to an actual order should be confirmed through the contact route before personal information or payment is supplied. Use the verified contact conversation to clarify who is responsible for your order and how to direct a privacy request.

## Information entered on the website
The channel search operates in your browser using the directory included with the page. Search terms are not sent to a search-service backend by this application. The trial selector and optional notes are held in the current page state to create a WhatsApp message; the application does not save them as a support ticket.

When you open a WhatsApp button, the selected message is included in the destination URL. WhatsApp or its application receives that navigation and handles the conversation under its own terms and privacy practices. Review the message before sending and avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

## Hosting and technical requests
Serving a website involves network requests to its hosting infrastructure. A hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser information, and access logs according to its configured practices. The final hosting operator, retention settings, and any additional services must be considered alongside this application-level notice.

Images and fonts are served as site assets in this implementation. The application does not embed payment processors, advertising pixels, or a third-party live-chat widget. Following an external reference or WhatsApp link moves you to a separate service whose practices are outside this website’s direct control.

## Optional analytics
The application includes a local event interface for interactions with WhatsApp buttons. It can report the button source and selected plan to an explicitly installed analytics adapter. Message contents, trial notes, and credentials are not included in that event payload. No external analytics adapter is enabled by the application as delivered.

If the operator later enables analytics, advertising, persistent cookies, or another data collection service, the notice and any required user controls need to reflect that implementation before collection starts. Supplying an analytics identifier alone does not silently activate tracking in this build.

## Information shared in a support conversation
A WhatsApp conversation may include a device model, service question, order reference, or other details you choose to send. Share only what is relevant to the request. Never send card security codes, online-banking passwords, or a full account URL in a public forum. A playlist address can include credentials and should be protected like a password.

The handling and retention of order conversations depend on the service operator and communications platform. Ask support about the applicable retention and deletion process before sharing information if that is material to your decision. The website does not promise a fixed retention period that has not been established by the operator.

## Privacy questions and requests
Use the [contact page](/contact) to raise a privacy question or request about information you provided. Describe the relevant conversation or order without sending more sensitive information than necessary. Support may need enough context to identify the request and explain the applicable process.

This notice should be reviewed when the site’s hosting, analytics, ordering workflow, or operator details materially change. The date shown on this page reflects the current authored version rather than an automatically refreshed daily date.
