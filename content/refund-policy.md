## Confirm refund conditions before paying
Before placing an order, ask KroozIPTV to confirm the cancellation and refund conditions that apply to the selected service, duration, payment method, and activation status. Retain that answer with the order reference. This website does not publish a guaranteed refund window or percentage because those operator terms have not been supplied as verified facts.

Do not assume that an unused period, a trial request, or a change in viewing preference automatically qualifies for a refund. Equally, this page does not state that every payment is non-refundable. The applicable order conditions and any relevant customer rights must be evaluated for the actual transaction.

## If activation or delivery is delayed
Contact support with the order reference, agreed duration, payment confirmation, and the activation timing stated in your conversation. Explain whether you have received account details and whether the account can authenticate. A delay in receiving instructions is different from a player that cannot be installed on a particular device.

Ask for the next step and expected handling of the issue. Do not resend payment simply because setup details have not arrived. Keep a record of the conversation and avoid sharing full payment-card details, banking passwords, or account credentials in public screenshots.

## If the service does not work as expected
Describe the exact symptom, device model, player version, connection type, affected content, and time. State whether every stream fails or one source is unavailable. A local Wi-Fi problem, unsupported player, inactive account, and missing guide data require different investigations.

Use the [troubleshooting guide](/blog/iptv-not-working) for controlled checks and share the results with support. You do not need to repeatedly factory-reset a device to provide a useful report. Preserve the working settings and record any change that materially affects playback.

## How to request a review
Use the refund or order-help route on the [contact page](/contact). Provide the order reference, purchase date, selected plan, a concise description of the issue, relevant evidence, and the resolution you are requesting. Include only the information necessary to identify the order and understand the problem.

Support needs to review the request against the confirmed terms and transaction details. Sending the message does not automatically approve a refund, cancel a plan, or set a processing date. Ask for written confirmation of the decision, any agreed amount, the payment route, and the expected timing if a refund is approved.

## Separate third-party purchases
A player license, app-store purchase, streaming device, or internet service may have been bought from another seller. Those purchases are governed by the relevant seller’s terms and are not automatically included in a KroozIPTV order review. Keep separate receipts so the correct party can evaluate the request.

If a player was recommended, still confirm who sells it and what its own trial or refund conditions are before paying. A service trial does not necessarily make a paid player license refundable. Test compatibility and clarify charges early to reduce avoidable uncertainty.

## Make an informed purchase
Use an available [trial](/free-trial), confirm essential [channel requests](/channels), and check the [device setup](/setup) before choosing a longer duration. Trial results describe the current experience; future availability and schedules can change. No plan duration guarantees permanent access to a particular event or feed.

Read the [terms](/terms), ask about any unresolved material conditions, and keep the written agreement. If you need clarification, ask support to explain the conditions for your specific order before making a commitment. Keep that explanation with the actual order confirmation.
